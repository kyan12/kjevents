# Style — `botanical`

**Essence.** Estate-garden wedding: ecru paper, moss and forest greens, terracotta
warmth, wildflower looseness under old trees. Daylight, air, unforced. For outdoor and
greenhouse weddings, garden parties, spring/summer celebrations.

## Mood lexicon

dappled shade · meadow loose · greenhouse glass · linen in wind · clay and terracotta ·
old stone wall · herb garden · long-table lunch light · pressed flowers · overcast soft

**Anti-lexicon:** tropical, jungle, neon greens, manicured, topiary, tuscan-kitsch.

## Light & materials

Open daylight — overcast-soft or dappled through leaves; never golden-hour amber
(that's classic-kj). Linen, raw clay, weathered wood, glass, stone; flowers loose and
garden-cut, stems visible, never florist-stiff domes.
