# Style — `noir`

**Essence.** Private members' club after midnight: plum-black lacquer, brass rails,
oxblood leather, candle pools in the dark. For milestone birthdays, supper clubs,
anniversary dinners with edge. Dark theme — ink is light-on-dark.

## Mood lexicon

low-key tungsten · brass and lacquer · oxblood leather banquette · cigar-lounge haze ·
upright bass · martini cart · velvet rope · last-call intimacy · film noir contrast

**Anti-lexicon:** neon, club lighting, LED, bottle service, glossy, party.

## Light & materials

Single warm tungsten pools in near-darkness; candle clusters; everything else falls
off to black. Lacquered wood, brass, smoked glass, leather, dark marble. Contrast is
the composition — protect true blacks, never lift shadows.

## Prompt templates
